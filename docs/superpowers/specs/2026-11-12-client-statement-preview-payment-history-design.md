# Design Spec: Client Statement PDF Preview & Payment History with Received Date (#157)

## Problem Statement
In GitHub Issue #157, users requested two key enhancements to the Client Ledger and Statement functionality:
1. **Statement & Aging PDF Preview Before Downloading:**
   - Currently, clicking "Statement PDF" or "Aging PDF" triggers an instant silent file download. Users must navigate away from the app to find and view the downloaded PDF.
   - Expected: An in-app PDF preview modal allowing merchants to view the generated document directly inside the software, with dedicated options to Download, Print, WhatsApp Share, and Close.
2. **Payment Received Date & Payment History in Client Ledger:**
   - Currently, the client ledger displays totals and invoice statuses, but does not show when payments were received, what payment modes were used, or chronological payment records per client.
   - Expected:
     - Show the **Latest Payment Date & Details** right in the client card header.
     - Add a dedicated **"Payment History"** tab under each client profile listing every payment received with date, invoice number, payment mode, reference ID/note, amount, and receipt.
     - In the **Client Statement PDF**, print the exact payment date, mode, and reference for each payment received instead of a generic message.
     - Allow recording payments directly from the client ledger without jumping back to Dashboard.

---

## Architecture & Components

### 1. Reusable In-App PDF Preview Modal (`src/components/PdfPreviewModal.jsx`)
- Accepts:
  - `title`: e.g., "Statement of Account — Ramesh Enterprises"
  - `filename`: e.g., `statement-ramesh-enterprises-2026-11-12.pdf`
  - `pdfBlob`: Binary Blob generated by `doc.output('blob')`
  - `clientPhone`: Optional phone number for WhatsApp sharing
  - `shareText`: Optional pre-composed WhatsApp message
  - `onClose`: Function to close the modal and revoke Blob URLs
- Capabilities:
  - Embedded PDF iframe preview (`<iframe src={blobUrl} />`) with responsive desktop & tablet sizing.
  - Action buttons:
    - **Download PDF** (`saveAs` or direct anchor download)
    - **Print** (triggers `iframe.contentWindow.print()` or popout print)
    - **WhatsApp Share** (opens WhatsApp web/app with pre-filled summary)
    - **Close** (destroys Blob URL and returns to ledger)

### 2. Client Payment History & Latest Payment Meta (`src/components/ClientsView.jsx`)
- **Latest Payment in Header:**
  - Aggregates all payments across client's bills (`bill.payments`).
  - Sorts descending by payment date (`p.date || p.recordedAt`).
  - Renders latest payment summary in the client card meta: e.g. `Latest payment: ₹2,000 on 05 Nov 2026 (UPI)`.
- **Client Detail Tab Switcher:**
  - Tabs inside expanded client card:
    - `Invoices (${clientBills.length})`
    - `Payment History (${clientPayments.length})`
- **Payment History Table:**
  - Date (dd/mm/yyyy)
  - Invoice No. badge
  - Mode (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, etc.) with badges
  - Reference / Note
  - Amount Received (₹ green bold)
  - Balance Due on Invoice
  - Receipt Action: Print / view receipt voucher
- **Record Payment Action:**
  - Green `₹` button on unpaid invoice rows in client bills table.
  - Allows recording payments directly from `ClientsView`.

### 3. Exact Payment Dates in Statement PDF (`ClientsView.jsx` & `PurchaseBills.jsx`)
- When generating the Client Statement PDF:
  - If a bill has recorded payments in `bill.payments`:
    - Loop over individual payment entries.
    - Print the actual payment date (`p.date`), mode (`p.mode`), and reference (`p.note`).
    - Debit/Credit columns and running balance accurately reflect each installment.
  - If marked paid without explicit entries, fallback to bill paid date.
